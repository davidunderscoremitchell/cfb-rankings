PAL (proven at least) is the main ranking: the low end of the range of theoretical records that fit a team's results.

Résumé is the theoretical record that best explains the team's results, given only who it beat and lost to.

Average is halfway between Résumé and PAL.

Strength is how well a team plays, judged with margins of victory, and it is used to rate opponents.

The Overall column can be sorted too, for plain records (win percentage, then wins, then PAL).

These rankings reward what teams actually did on the field: wins and losses against their real schedule, with every opponent judged by a margin-based Strength rating. Records alone ignore who you played, and Strength alone measures how you played rather than what you achieved; PAL and Résumé combine the two.
